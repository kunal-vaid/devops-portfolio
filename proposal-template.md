## Rules that make this work

- **First line must mirror their problem**, not your resume. The client should
  see their words back.
- **Match their stack exactly.** If they say ArgoCD, mention ArgoCD — don't
  substitute your favorite equivalent.
- **Ask one real question.** It shows you read the post and gives them an easy
  reason to reply.
- **Skip gigs with 50+ proposals at $15/hr.** Your time is better spent on 2
  well-written proposals than 20 sprayed ones.
- **Attach portfolio links** (this repo) on fixed-price gigs — clients check.
